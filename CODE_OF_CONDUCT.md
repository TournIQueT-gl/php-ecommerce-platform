# Community code of conduct

We welcome constructive contributions from people of different experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss the work, not the person. Be respectful, provide reproducible information, give credit and avoid harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory behavior.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to keep the project safe. For sensitive concerns, contact the repository owner privately instead of posting personal data in public issues.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker.
